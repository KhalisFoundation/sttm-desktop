How it's scored

Actual is the pangti being sung or read (answer key). Each version's row is what Gurbani Autopilot put on screen. The thin strip under it is green when the right pangti was shown (or the screen moved to it less than 1 s ago), amber for the right Shabad but another pangti, red for a wrong Shabad, faint for nothing shown or no answer key.

Accuracy = share of scored seconds with the right pangti; where only a Shabad answer key exists, share with the right Shabad. Practice clips only; the held-out test set is never shown.
